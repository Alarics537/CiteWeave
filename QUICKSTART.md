# 研织使用说明 · CiteWeave quick guide

| | 中文 | English |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **安装**　Windows 双击 MSI，从开始菜单打开研织。M 系列 Mac（macOS 14.4 或更高版本）解压 ZIP，把 CiteWeave 拖入「应用程序」。Mac 版为预览版，尚未公证。 | **Install**　On Windows, run the MSI and open CiteWeave from Start. On an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14.4 or later, unzip the download and drag CiteWeave into Applications. The Mac preview is not notarized. |
| 2 | **添加文献**　在「文献库」选择「添加文件夹」，选取本地 PDF 目录。研织会扫描子文件夹并监测新文献。单击条目可在右侧核对题名、作者和期刊。 | **Add papers**　Open Library, choose Add folder, and select your PDF directory. CiteWeave scans subfolders and watches for new papers. Select a paper to review its title, authors, and journal. |
| 3 | **阅读和记录**　双击文献打开 PDF。选中文字可高亮，阅读侧栏可添加笔记。「知识库」可保存你对一篇或多篇文献的理解。 | **Read and take notes**　Double-click a paper to open its PDF. Select text to highlight it or use the reading panel to add a note. Use the knowledge base for notes that draw on one or more papers. |
| 4 | **写作与引用**　在总览新建写作项目。在文稿中放好光标，点「插入引用」，到文献库选择文献并确认。在辅助面板调整期刊样式，完成后点「导出 Word」。 | **Write and cite**　Create a writing project from the overview. Place the cursor in your draft, choose Insert citation, select papers in the library, and confirm. Pick a journal style in the side panel and export to Word. |
| 5 | **保存与更新**　查看写作台的工作稿保存状态。大改前可在「版本」中保存快照。「个人工作区」提供整库备份和「检查更新」。 | **Save and update**　Check the draft save status in the writing desk. Save a named version before a major revision. Open Personal workspace for backups and Check for updates. |

需要 AI 辅助时，在「个人工作区」连接 ChatGPT，即可从文献或写作工具中调用。  
For AI assistance, connect ChatGPT in Personal workspace, then use the AI tools while reading or writing.
